# Signals

Generated by `scripts/gen_signals_doc.py` from the registry. Do not edit by hand.

Each signal is winsorized at the 1st and 99th percentiles, sign-aligned so that higher means higher expected return, and z-scored per rebalance date. A date is dropped for a signal when it covers under 50% of the universe or under 100 names.

Month 0 is the month ending at the rebalance date. Spec months `t-12` to `t-2` count from holding month `t`, so `mom_12_1` covers months 11 to 1 and skips the latest month.
